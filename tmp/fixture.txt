Automated Fault Detection in Rooftop Solar Arrays

Abstract
This report investigates whether thermal imaging can replace manual inspection of
rooftop photovoltaic arrays in tropical climates.

1. Introduction
Deep learning always outperforms threshold methods for photovoltaic fault classification [1].
Thermal anomaly detection identifies cell cracking before power output degrades [2].
